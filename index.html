Crash-Risk Dashboard

A personal Google Apps Script that scores equity market risk from public data.

The app monitors publicly available macroeconomic, credit, valuation and market data, combines it into a single 1–10 risk score, and emails a daily summary to its owner.

What it does

Every hour it reads public data — Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), daily ETF closes, and a small number of public market indicators — writes the results into a Google Sheet owned by the script’s owner, and scores eight risk domains: valuation, yield curve, credit, volatility, breadth, macro, liquidity, and the share of S&P 500 constituents above their 200-day moving average. Once a day it emails a summary to the owner.

Who it is for

One person: the owner of the spreadsheet it is bound to. It is not a service, it has no other users, no sign-up, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Not financial advice
