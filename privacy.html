Privacy Policy — Crash-Risk Dashboard

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Crash-Risk Dashboard ("the app") is a personal Google Apps Script used solely by its owner and developer. It has no other users and provides no service to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

The app runs under the Google account of its owner and accesses only that account's own data:

  • Google Sheets — reads and writes the single spreadsheet the script is bound to, which holds the app's configuration, fetched data, scores and history.
  • trong>Gmail / Mail — sends the daily summary email from the owner's account to the owner's own address. It does not read, search, or modify any existing mail.
  • trong>External requests — fetches publicly available financial and economic data from third-party sources, including the Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) API, Yahoo Finance, Cboe, FINRA, and similar public sources. These requests send no personal data; they are ordinary public data lookups.
  • trong>Script properties and triggers — stores small operational values (cached readings, timestamps, state flags) inside the script's own storage, and schedules the app's own recurring runs.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or disclose personal data to any third party.
  • does not read the owner's email, contacts, calendar, or files other than the one spreadsheet it is bound to.
  • has no analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
  • has no other users, accounts, or sign-up, and therefore holds no user data belonging to anyone else.

Where data is stored

All data the app produces stays inside the owner's own Google account — in the bound Google Sheet and in the script's own property storage. Nothing is stored anywhere else, and no external database or server is operated by the app.

Retention and deletion

Data persists in the owner's spreadsheet for as long as the owner keeps it. The owner can delete all of it at any time by deleting the spreadsheet and the script project, and can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published in this file, with the date above updated.
